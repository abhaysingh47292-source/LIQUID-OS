✋ 1. Hand & Gesture Controls (Spatial Interface)
To use this, click the "ENABLE HAND CONTROL" button in the top right corner. Ensure you are running the file on a local server (localhost) and allow camera permissions.

Open Palm: Generates a powerful repulsion field. Move your hand to push particles away and clear a path.

Closed Fist: Generates a gravitational vortex. Pulls particles tightly toward your hand and swirls them around.

Pinch (Thumb + Index Finger): Precision grab. Particles will cluster tightly at the exact point of your pinch, allowing you to drag the center of gravity.

Swipe (Rapid horizontal movement): Detects a quick palm swipe to instantly destabilize the current shape and randomly morph into a new one.

Two Hands: Bring both hands into the frame. The distance between your hands controls the scale of the entire particle engine (move hands apart to expand, together to shrink).

🎙️ 2. Voice Commands
Click the Microphone Icon (🎤) next to the command bar at the bottom. Allow microphone permissions. Speak naturally.

"Show [Shape]": (e.g., "Show galaxy", "Show DNA", "Show blackhole")

"Create [Shape]": (e.g., "Create heart", "Create atom")

"Theme [Color]": (e.g., "Theme neon", "Theme matrix")

"Reset": Restores the default cyan galaxy.

"Fullscreen": Expands the interface.

Note: You can also just speak a single word, and if it isn't a command, the particles will form that word.

⌨️ 3. Command Console (Text Input)
Click the input box at the bottom (> ENTER COMMAND OR SHAPE...) and press Enter after typing.

Available Shapes:

galaxy

blackhole

dna

sphere

atom

cube

heart

matrix (Falling digital rain)

void (Deep space particle spread)

Available Themes:

theme cyan (Default)

theme violet

theme amber

theme matrix (Green)

theme white

theme ice (Light blue)

theme neon (Hot pink)

System Modifiers:

particles [number]: Changes the particle density in real-time. (e.g., type particles 80000). Note: Max is 100,000.

reset: Resets system to defaults.

Custom Text Rendering: Type any unrecognized word (e.g., HELLO or SYSTEM) and the particles will physically morph to spell out that word.
